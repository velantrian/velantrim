# Adversarial Audit — Relation Semantics
## Velantrim Unified Cognitive System Architecture (Working Master, doc 1lPtn06ugz-csvbjbbBYRRMXSDG3VIDFvoyhQ0X6mbWk, as of 2026-09-03)

This audit examines only what the document's arrows and relation-words actually denote: whether causal, control, constitutive, representational, evidential, and normative relations stay distinguishable when the document narrates its own internal dynamics, whether "type of relation" and "status/provenance of a relation" are kept genuinely orthogonal, and whether the chained-relations material overclaims general non-transitivity. The document is unusually self-aware about this exact failure mode in the abstract (its own relation-type list at lines 254-269, and its SOURCE≠REPRESENTATION≠CLAIM≠EVIDENCE≠BELIEF≠KNOWLEDGE chain, are genuinely well-built). The findings below are about where that discipline is *not* actually applied — especially to the document's own diagrams and to its own account of how Essence/Memory/Relations feed back into Thinking and Perception.

### Finding 1 — The map's own arrows are exactly the undifferentiated "связано" the prose warns against

**SOURCE:** The top-level architecture map (lines 13–34) connects ВОСПРИЯТИЕ, БЫСТРОЕ СХВАТЫВАНИЕ, ПАМЯТЬ, СВЯЗИ ЗНАНИЙ, МЫШЛЕНИЕ, СУТЬ, ДОСТАТОЧНО?, УГЛУБИТЬСЯ, ПРОТИВОРЕЧИЕ, НЕИЗВЕСТНОЕ using only `↕`, `↙↘`, `←──→`, `↓`, `└──→` glyphs. The only disclaimer given is "Это карта отношений, а не обязательный порядок исполнения" (line 14) — it rules out reading the arrows as a mandatory execution sequence, but says nothing about what kind of relation each arrow *is* instead.

**INFERENCE:** The document spends enormous effort insisting that "связано" (connected) is not a usable predicate for object-level cognition — RELATION LABEL ≠ PROVEN RELATION, SIMILARITY ≠ MECHANISM, one arrow "не позволяет мышлению понять, что из этой связи разрешено выводить" (line 270). But the map's own arrows are precisely that: unlabeled connectivity, with directionality but no relation-type. The one disclaimer supplied (not-a-sequence) rules out a single misreading without supplying any positive account of what the arrow *does* mean — the same "A≠B with no positive B" gap the document elsewhere criticizes as an epistemic hazard when memory or relations do it. This is the document failing its own guardrail reflexively.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (constitutive-rules/protocol domain):** A board-game rulebook diagram with an arrow from "draw card" to "score changes" is ambiguous between a *procedural* relation (this step follows that step) and a *causal/numeric* relation (drawing this card causes this many points to be added) — and rulebooks that leave this ambiguous produce real rules disputes at the table, because "the arrow means something happened next" and "the arrow means this determines that outcome" license entirely different player actions. **(Medical/diagnostic domain):** A clinical flowchart arrow from "symptom observed" to "test ordered" is ambiguous between *temporal sequence* (this was next), *evidential warrant* (this symptom is evidence favoring this test), and *authorization* (observing this symptom licenses ordering this test under a protocol) — conflating the third with the first two is exactly the failure the document itself names elsewhere as НУЖНО УЗНАТЬ ≠ РАЗРЕШЕНО ДЕЙСТВОВАТЬ (line 476), but its own diagram vocabulary can't express that distinction visually.

**VERDICT:** partially survives. The "not a mandatory sequence" disclaimer is real and useful, but it removes only the temporal-necessity misreading; it does not supply, and nothing else in the front layer supplies, a positive account of what an arrow *does* assert. No new ontology is needed to fix this — the document already possesses a relation-type list (lines 254-269); the fix is to apply it, or at minimum flag that the diagram's arrows are typed no more precisely than "these two things matter to each other," which is weaker than most of the prose implies.

### Finding 2 — "ОБРАТНОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ" collapses causal, control, and constitutive relations into one verb, and one of them overclaims

**SOURCE:** Lines 79–81, presented as a single flat list titled "ОБРАТНОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ" (reverse influence):
- "Суть меняет то, на что мы смотрим дальше" ("changes")
- "Быстрое схватывание направляет новые запросы к памяти" ("directs")
- "Связи определяют, что память сможет поднять по ассоциации" ("determine")
Reinforced at line 39 ("могут влиять на мышление и восприятие постоянно") and line 373 ("Суть может влиять обратно: на внимание, на выбор памяти, на то, куда углубляться и что считать важным").

**INFERENCE:** Three different verbs are doing three different jobs, unflagged as different: (a) "меняет" for Essence→attention reads as a *causal/attentional-control* relation — Essence sets a parameter of an attention process; (b) "направляет" for Fast-grasping→query reads as *control over query formation* — a different control target than (a); (c) "определяют" for Relations→retrievability is stronger than either — it is a *constitutive/enabling-condition* claim: relations don't merely bias what memory surfaces, they set the *possibility space* of what can be surfaced at all. That third claim is materially stronger than "influence," yet is grouped under the same "ОБРАТНОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ" heading as the two softer, control-type claims, with no signal that one of the three is a gatekeeping claim and the other two are steering claims.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (empirical/investigative domain, extending the document's own dry-land example at lines 330-336):** An investigator's working essence is "drought." Under (c) as literally stated, the investigator's *existing relations* determine what memory can raise by association — meaning an "arson" precedent that has no existing drought-adjacent link in memory becomes structurally unretrievable, not merely less likely to surface, precisely because "определяют" (determine) was used rather than "влияет" (bias). That is a stronger and more dangerous claim than the document's own later, much more careful treatment of the identical worry in "ФОНОВАЯ ПАМЯТЬ" (lines 210–221), which explicitly frames it as a tunable strength ("если фоновая память слишком сильна... если слабая...") rather than a hard determination. The two passages describe the same mechanism with incompatible strength claims and neither cross-references the other.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (resource-bounded inquiry domain):** A security analyst with ten minutes to triage an alert has fast-grasping "направлять" (direct) her query to memory — a soft steering claim she can override. If instead Relations "определяют" (determine) what her memory search can surface, she has no way to know that a genuinely relevant unlabeled precedent is being categorically excluded rather than merely under-weighted — the two readings call for opposite mitigations (override a bias vs. force an unindexed full-text search), and the document's single "ОБРАТНОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ" framing doesn't tell her which is at stake.

**VERDICT:** partially survives. The underlying phenomena (essence redirects attention, fast-grasping shapes queries, relations gate associative recall) are all legitimate and worth naming. The problem is that a stronger constitutive/gatekeeping claim ("определяют") is presented as equivalent in kind to two softer control/steering claims ("меняет," "направляет") under one heading, and that stronger claim is inconsistent with the hedged, non-absolute treatment the same risk receives fourteen lines earlier in the document. No new primitive is required — the existing distinction between "influences" (probabilistic/control) and "determines/constitutes" (sets the possibility space) already exists implicitly in the document's vocabulary elsewhere; it should simply be applied consistently here rather than flattened into "ОБРАТНОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ."

### Finding 3 — A≠B guardrails almost never specify the corresponding positive license

**SOURCE:** A representative sample of the document's own inequalities, none paired with a positive statement of what the left side *does* license:
- "ИЗВЛЕЧЕНО ИЗ ПАМЯТИ ≠ ДОКАЗАНО" / "ПОХОЖЕ ≠ ТО ЖЕ САМОЕ" (103–104)
- "SIMILARITY ≠ MECHANISM," "CORRELATION ≠ CAUSATION" (284–285)
- "COHERENT NETWORK ≠ TRUE MODEL," "MORE CONNECTIONS ≠ MORE UNDERSTANDING," "MISSING RELATION ≠ NO RELATION IN REALITY" (354–356)
Contrast with two places where the document *does* supply a positive account: "ANALOGY → CANDIDATE UNDERSTANDING" immediately following "ANALOGY ≠ ESTABLISHED MECHANISM" (328–329), and the transformation-type passage: "Compression удаляет или сворачивает различия; aggregation объединяет основания; inference / derivation создают новый вывод; corroboration может усиливать поддержку через независимые основания" (746).

**INFERENCE:** The negative guardrail pattern (A≠B) is applied dozens of times across the memory, relations, and expectation sections, but the positive question — "if similarity doesn't license mechanism, what, if anything, does a similarity claim license, and under what condition?" — is answered explicitly in only one or two places (analogy, and the compression/aggregation/inference/derivation/corroboration list). Everywhere else, a thinker is told what a signal *cannot* be used for but not what it *can* be used for, which leaves "what do I do with this now" genuinely undetermined — this is a real gap, not a stylistic quirk, because it recurs at the volume it does. Note this is a strength as well as a weakness: the two exceptions prove the document is capable of doing this well when it tries; it simply doesn't try consistently.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (resource-bounded inquiry domain):** A malware analyst matches a new binary's behavior to a known family ("SIMILARITY ≠ MECHANISM," correctly, since string/behavior similarity doesn't prove shared code lineage). Under time pressure she needs to know: does the similarity alone license (a) provisional triage priority, (b) a specific follow-up test, (c) nothing beyond noting it as a candidate? The document's guardrail blocks the overclaim but gives her no scaffold for the underclaim question, unlike the analogy passage's "→ CANDIDATE UNDERSTANDING," which does answer exactly this for the analogy case.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (medical/diagnostic domain):** "ОТСУТСТВИЕ КОНТРПРИМЕРА ≠ УНИВЕРСАЛЬНОСТЬ ПРАВИЛА" (line 204) correctly blocks a clinician from treating "no exception on file" as "the rule has no exceptions." But it doesn't say what the absence of a recorded exception *does* support — e.g., does it support a weak prior update, or nothing at all given how the record was built? The "передача скрытой ошибки" example three paragraphs earlier (190-198) shows the document is aware this can be *actively misleading*, which makes the missing positive account more consequential here than in a case where the null result is merely uninformative.

**VERDICT:** partially survives. The negative guardrails are correct as far as they go and are clearly load-bearing for the document's anti-overclaim stance. But as a general pattern, "tells you what it doesn't license, not what it does" recurs pervasively enough to be a structural gap rather than an occasional omission — and the document's own two counterexamples (analogy; compression/aggregation/inference/derivation/corroboration) show the fix doesn't require any new entity, just extending a technique already demonstrated in the text.

### Finding 4 — "Contested" is listed as a peer of provenance items, but it is a different, orthogonal axis

**SOURCE:** Lines 271–280, under the heading "СВЯЗЬ И ЕЁ СТАТУС — НЕ ОДНО И ТО ЖЕ" ("relation and its status are not the same thing"), status is presented as one flat bulleted alternative-set: прямо наблюдаемой; явно заявленной источником; выведенной из других отношений; предложенной как рабочая гипотеза; перенесённой по аналогии; унаследованной из прошлого опыта; **спорной или конфликтующей с другой моделью**.

**INFERENCE:** The first six items all answer one question — "how did this relation come to be held" (a genealogy/acquisition axis). The seventh, "contested/conflicting," answers a different question — "does this relation currently have disputed standing" — which is a social/dialectical axis, not an acquisition-path axis. These two axes are not mutually exclusive alternatives; they're independent. A relation can be directly-observed *and* contested, or inferred *and* uncontested. By listing "contested" as a seventh item in the same flat enumeration as the acquisition-path items, the document implies picking one item fully characterizes a relation's status, when in fact a relation needs at least two independent tags (how-acquired, and current-dispute-state) to be honestly characterized. This is exactly the kind of axis-conflation my brief asks me to check for — and it occurs in the very passage whose heading explicitly claims to be establishing an orthogonal distinction (type vs. status), one level down inside "status" itself.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (medical/diagnostic domain):** Two radiologists look at the same CT scan. Both directly observe the same image data (status: "прямо наблюдаемой" for both), yet they disagree on whether a shadow is a tumor (status: "спорной"). Under the document's flat list, this case has no clean slot — "directly observed" and "contested" are presented as alternatives, but the actual epistemic situation is "directly observed, and also contested at the interpretation layer." A system trying to log this using the document's status list as written would have to pick one tag and lose the other, silently re-creating exactly the kind of information loss the surrounding sections are trying to prevent.

**VERDICT:** partially survives → rejected on this specific sub-point. NO NEW PRIMITIVE REQUIRED — the fix is not a new axis-taxonomy, it's a one-sentence clarification that "contested/conflicting" is a cross-cutting property that can co-occur with any of the other six acquisition-path items, not a seventh peer in the same enumeration. The heading's intent (type ≠ status) is correct; the execution one level down re-introduces the very kind of flattening the heading warns against.

### Finding 5 — Type and status are not actually a free cross-product: some types structurally forbid some statuses, unflagged

**SOURCE:** The relation-type list includes "ПРИЧИННОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ" (causal influence) as a type (line 262). The status list separately includes "прямо наблюдаемой" (directly observed) as a status (line 274), with nothing restricting which types may carry which statuses. Two sections later: "CORRELATION ≠ CAUSATION" (line 285) is asserted as a firewall.

**INFERENCE:** If type and status are presented as fully orthogonal, independently-choosable axes (as the "не одно и то же" framing implies), nothing in the document prevents tagging a causal-influence-type relation with status "directly observed." But that is precisely what the CORRELATION≠CAUSATION guardrail says cannot honestly happen — under a standard (Humean) reading, causation itself is never directly observed, only succession/co-occurrence is; causal status is always inferred, hypothesized, or inherited, never observed outright. The document's two devices (an open type×status cross-product, and the correlation/causation firewall) are in unflagged tension: taken literally, the cross-product allows a tagging combination that the firewall forbids.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (empirical/descriptive domain, general form of the correlation problem):** An analyst watching a dashboard sees metric A rise immediately before metric B rises, repeatedly. If she is permitted, under the document's own type/status scheme, to record the relation-type as "ПРИЧИННОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ" with status "прямо наблюдаемой," she has smuggled causation in as an observation rather than an inference — exactly the error the firewall exists to prevent — and nothing in the status-list section itself stops her, since that section never cross-references the causation guardrail defined elsewhere.

**VERDICT:** unresolved. This isn't a case where the document is simply wrong — the correlation/causation firewall is correct, and the type/status distinction is a good instinct. But the two are never reconciled: the document doesn't state that some relation-types (causal influence, mechanism) have a restricted status-range (never "directly observed," always at best "inferred" or "hypothesis"), while others (identity, temporal relation) may legitimately be directly observed. NO NEW PRIMITIVE REQUIRED — this is a one-sentence scope note on the existing type/status pairing, not a new construct.

### Finding 6 — The chained-relations claim is correct for loose "связано" but oversteps into domains where composition IS guaranteed by definition or rule

**SOURCE:** "Если A связано с B, а B связано с C, это ещё не означает, что A тем же образом связано с C" (line 315), followed by four examples (similarity-chaining, temporal-succession-as-causation, part-whole transfer, context-bound-rule merging), concluding "мышление должно... проверять, СОХРАНЯЕТСЯ ЛИ ТИП И СМЫСЛ СВЯЗИ при каждом переходе" (321).

**INFERENCE:** The four worked examples are all well-chosen and genuinely non-transitive relations (similarity, mere succession, part-whole role-transfer, scope-bound rules). But the section states its conclusion as a general instruction to *always* re-check composition "at each hop," using the single generic Russian verb "связано" for all four cases — which risks being read as a substrate-neutral law about relations-in-general, exactly the kind of generalization this audit is asked to test. Some relations compose by *definition or by rule*, not merely as an empirical regularity that happens to hold: mathematical equality and strict implication are transitive because transitivity is part of what those relations *are*; many institutional relations are transitive by explicit constitutive rule.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (formal mathematics):** If A = B and B = C, then A = C follows necessarily from the definition of equality — no re-verification of "type and meaning at each hop" is epistemically required, because the composition is guaranteed by the relation's own axioms. Applying the document's slogan literally here would add unwarranted doubt to a domain where none is warranted — an overclaim in the opposite direction from the one the document is trying to prevent.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (constitutive-rules/legal domain):** If statute S validly delegates rule-making authority to agency X, and agency X's regulation validly sub-delegates specific authority to officer O, then O's action is authorized under S — this transitivity is constitutive of how delegation chains are *designed* to work in many legal systems (and is precisely how corporate signing authority, power-of-attorney chains, and treaty-accession chains are meant to compose). What must be checked at each hop is *validity* of each individual link (was the delegation properly executed), not whether the *relation type itself* (authorization) survives composition — the type is stipulated to survive by rule. The document's framing, read generally, would tell an analyst to doubt something that the legal system in question has already settled by design.

**VERDICT:** survives with scope. The caution is genuinely valuable for empirically/causally/analogically grounded relations (similarity, correlation, causal influence, analogy, "связано" in its loose everyday sense) — precisely the relations the rest of the document is built around. It does not hold, and is not scoped as not holding, for relations that are transitive by definition (mathematical equality, subset, strict implication) or by constitutive rule (many legal/institutional delegation and inheritance chains). This is exactly the kind of "survives in one domain, not substrate-neutral as stated" case the audit brief asks to be called out explicitly: the slogan as written is not a general law about "relations," it is a (correct) law about *empirically/inductively grounded* relation-chains, and the document should say so rather than let "связано" stand in for all relation composition.

### Finding 7 — The document's own crisper relation-discipline lives in the layer marked "not binding," while the collapses happen in the layer marked "current and authoritative"

**SOURCE:** The front layer is introduced as "текущая рабочая архитектурная карта" (line 13, current working map) and is where Findings 1–2 occur. The legacy layer is explicitly demoted: "старые owner/runtime/technology формулировки не надо автоматически переносить в новый каркас" (line 840). Yet it is the legacy layer that contains the document's *most* careful relation-type discipline: "SOURCE ≠ REPRESENTATION ≠ CLAIM ≠ EVIDENCE ≠ BELIEF ≠ KNOWLEDGE" (870), "RETRIEVAL ≠ EVIDENCE. RANK ≠ AUTHORITY" (878), "COMPRESSION ≠ AGGREGATION ≠ INFERENCE ≠ DERIVATION ≠ CORROBORATION" (745).

**INFERENCE:** This is a changing-target case in miniature: the document itself is the thing that changes over time (research rounds are explicitly layered on, per lines 563–564, 732–733), and the document's own stated policy for such change (revision must not erase or silently override earlier-established distinctions — "ПЕРЕСМОТР НЕ СТИРАЕТ ИСТОРИЮ," line 510) is arguably not being followed for its own relation-semantics discipline: the sharpest existing tools for keeping representational/evidential/control relations apart (RANK≠AUTHORITY, SOURCE≠REPRESENTATION≠...≠KNOWLEDGE) were built in the layer now marked non-binding, and were not restated or cross-referenced when the front layer's own "ОБРАТНОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ" and top-map prose were written or revised. The document warns readers not to let compiled/compressed material silently increase in status ("COMPRESSION ALONE MUST NOT SILENTLY INCREASE EPISTEMIC STRENGTH," line 742) but doesn't apply the mirror-image caution to its own layering: demoting the legacy layer to "reference only" must not silently *decrease* the discipline attached to a distinction the front layer still needs.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (changing-world domain, applied reflexively to the document):** Suppose a future editor, following the instruction to treat the legacy layer as non-binding, reads only the front layer and revises "Связи определяют, что память сможет поднять по ассоциации" (F2) without ever encountering "RETRIEVAL ≠ EVIDENCE. RANK ≠ AUTHORITY" from the legacy layer three sections later than where it would be needed. Nothing in the front layer flags that this specific legacy distinction is exactly the tool needed to fix F2. The document's own anti-drift checklist (lines 830-837) asks "не слили ли два разных различения в один удобный термин?" — but that self-check is run against the front layer's own recent additions, not against whether a legacy-layer distinction that would resolve a front-layer collapse has gone unconsulted.

**VERDICT:** unresolved. This is not a factual error so much as a structural risk the document creates for itself by design (working master vs. reference layer). Flagging it doesn't require a new module — it requires the front layer's arrow-language (F1, F2) to actually cite the relevant legacy distinctions it already possesses (RANK≠AUTHORITY, SOURCE≠REPRESENTATION≠CLAIM≠EVIDENCE) rather than reinventing looser prose that the legacy layer had already disciplined.

## Closing: what survives cleanly vs. what's genuinely broken

**Survives cleanly (real strengths, not just restated guardrails):**
- The core relation-type catalog at lines 254–269 (identity/part-whole/dependency/condition/constraint/causal-influence/mechanism/temporal/function/support-opposition/similarity/structural-analogy/boundary) keeps causal influence and mechanism, and constraint and condition, genuinely distinct as *types* — this list does not collapse under the pressure it's later subjected to elsewhere in the prose.
- ANALOGY → CANDIDATE UNDERSTANDING (line 328) is a rare, correctly-built positive-license statement paired with its A≠B guardrail — proof the document can do this when it tries.
- COMPRESSION ≠ AGGREGATION ≠ INFERENCE ≠ DERIVATION ≠ CORROBORATION (745–746) is a well-differentiated account of five distinct relation-generating operations with a positive gloss for each — the strongest single passage in the document for this audit's purposes.
- RANKING ≠ AUTHORITY (875) and Titan/Crystal role-separation (879) correctly keep a control-type relation (order of attention) distinct from a normative one (epistemic authority) — this is exactly the discipline missing from the front-layer "ОБРАТНОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ" material.
- The general instinct behind chained-relations non-composition is correct for the inductive/similarity/causal relations the document is actually built around (Finding 6) — it just needs scope language, not rejection.

**Genuinely broken or unresolved:**
- The document's own diagrams use untyped arrows for exactly the "связано" predicate its prose spends pages discrediting (Finding 1).
- "ОБРАТНОЕ ВЛИЯНИЕ" mixes a constitutive/gatekeeping claim with two softer control claims under one verb, and is inconsistent in strength with the more careful treatment of the same worry elsewhere (Finding 2).
- A≠B guardrails are pervasively one-sided — negative without positive — well beyond the two places that do it right (Finding 3).
- "Contested" is folded into a provenance/status list as if it were a peer acquisition-path item rather than an orthogonal current-standing property (Finding 4).
- Type and status are treated as a free cross-product with no flag that some types (causal influence) structurally restrict which statuses are honest (Finding 5) — this directly threatens the document's own correlation/causation firewall.
- The chained-relations slogan, if read as general rather than scoped to inductively-grounded relations, overclaims against formal-mathematical and legal/institutional relations that compose by definition or rule (Finding 6).
- The document's best relation-discipline tools sit in the layer explicitly marked non-binding, while the collapses occur in the layer marked current — a self-inflicted structural risk under the document's own stated policy that revision must not silently lose ground (Finding 7).
