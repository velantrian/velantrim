# Adversarial Audit — Experience / Attention / Selection Edge

This audit focuses on the document's treatment of experience-as-readiness, the resolution-allocation reframing of experience, the routine/baseline guardrails, the person/user-model self-confirming-loop open question, and the fast/slow orient-then-deepen machinery (current-frame layer + legacy §6/§21). I read the full 1006-line export directly. My overall finding: the document is unusually careful, at the level of stated principle, about *not* treating "didn't select/attend to X" as "X doesn't exist" — that guardrail family recurs independently across at least four sections. Where it is genuinely thin is upstream of that: it has not cleanly separated four different things it calls by overlapping names ("опыт," "разрешение," "внимание"), and its one explicit self-confirming-loop worry is correctly flagged as *open* but is not, in fact, blocked anywhere else in the text — including by the one candidate mechanism (offline consolidation) that could plausibly block it, which the document itself defers to an unplaced "horizon."

---

## Finding 1 — Four distinct things travel under one label, "распределение разрешения"

**SOURCE:** Line 813: "опыт может улучшать распределение разрешения — что можно понимать через общую схему, что надо держать подробнее... где нужно selectively reopen." Lines 651–660 repeat the same list under "ОПЫТ → ВОЗМОЖНО, ЛУЧШЕЕ РАСПРЕДЕЛЕНИЕ РАЗРЕШЕНИЯ." Legacy §21 (lines 934–937) frames resource allocation ("increase reasoning effort, retrieval depth... when the current model is insufficient") as a response to a single question's insufficiency, not as an allocation across competing simultaneous demands.

**INFERENCE:** One phrase covers at least four different things without separating them: (a) accumulated dispositional readiness (what will be recognized faster later — a property of the updated system), (b) real-time selection of what to deepen into now for the current question, (c) what gets folded back into future readiness afterward, and (d) hard resource scarcity — the fact that depth spent on one thread is depth *not* spent on another live thread at the same time. §21 treats effort as elastic-on-demand ("increase... when insufficient") with no acknowledgment that two simultaneously-insufficient models might compete for the same finite budget.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (resource-bounded inquiry):** An ER triage nurse (or on-call analyst under SLA) faces two moderately concerning cases at once: one matches a well-drilled pattern (readiness pays off — fast, correct), one is genuinely novel and would need real deepening to even characterize. Real time is fixed. Giving depth to the novel case isn't "my current model is insufficient" in §21's single-question sense — it's a forced trade-off against the *other* live, also-material demand. Speed gained on the familiar case (readiness) doesn't automatically free capacity for the novel one; that freed time could just as easily be consumed by a third demand. The document's "deepen where insufficient" logic has no answer for *which* insufficiency wins when several compete for the same scarce budget.

**VERDICT:** partially survives. The individual pieces are each gestured at somewhere (§16 Experience/Learning vs. §15 Progressive Understanding vs. §21 Resource Allocation), so no piece is entirely missing — but they are never explicitly separated as four independent axes, and concurrent-competing-demand scarcity is not modeled at all, only single-question insufficiency. Given the document's own stated worry (line 833) about not "quietly merging two different distinctions into one convenient term," "распределение разрешения" as currently used is itself an example of exactly that.

## Finding 2 — The self-confirming personalization loop is named as an open question but not blocked anywhere

**SOURCE:** Person/user-model boundary, open question #5 (line 730): "Как не сделать personalization самоподтверждающейся петлёй: система показывает человеку только то, что соответствует старой модели, а затем принимает его реакцию на этот узкий выбор как новое подтверждение модели?" Nearby guardrails (lines 697–701): USER MODEL ≠ AUTHORITY OVER USER; ПОКАЗАТЬ ПОСЛЕДСТВИЯ ≠ ЗАКРЫТЬ ВАРИАНТ; ВЫСОКАЯ УВЕРЕННОСТЬ ≠ ПРАВО ОТМЕНИТЬ ВЫБОР. The revision loop (line 712): "ПРОШЛЫЙ ПАТТЕРН → ОЖИДАНИЕ → НОВОЕ ПОВЕДЕНИЕ / ВЫБОР → СОПОСТАВЛЕНИЕ → ПЕРЕСМОТР ПРИ НЕОБХОДИМОСТИ."

**INFERENCE:** Every nearby guardrail constrains *authority over an already-presented choice* — the system must not decide for the user, must not foreclose a shown option, must not treat confidence as license to override. None of them constrain the earlier step: what gets selected for presentation in the first place. The self-confirming loop lives entirely upstream of "authority," in the candidate-generation step — the user is never denied a choice, they simply were never shown the wider space. The revision loop (line 712) implicitly assumes "новое поведение / выбор" arrives from an unbiased channel; if the model itself sets the visible option set, that channel is not unbiased, and СОПОСТАВЛЕНИЕ will structurally under-produce disconfirmation no matter how honestly it's run.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (user interaction / performative feedback):** An assistant models a user as "prefers concise, low-risk options" and defaults to surfacing only conservative suggestions — not lying, just never raising ambitious ones. The user, seeing only conservative options, keeps picking conservative options; each pick is logged as "confirmed: user still prefers low-risk." The user's actual interests may have shifted substantially, but nothing in the specified loop ever asks "is my candidate-generation itself narrowed by the model under test?" Every prediction keeps coming true from the system's point of view.

**VERDICT:** unresolved (and correctly self-labeled as such — it's item 5 of an open-questions list, not a solved edge). No passage supplies a mechanism that would break the loop (e.g., deliberately surfacing a currently-suppressed option, or distinguishing "confirmed" from "never tested"); only the aspiration "should better reveal the decision space" (line 704) is offered, which is a value statement, not a check. This is precisely the pattern the audit brief predicted: a guardrail sentence sits nearby but does not actually cover the failure mode it looks adjacent to.

## Finding 3 — The identical structural gap recurs at the background-memory edge, and the one candidate fix is explicitly parked outside the current architecture

**SOURCE:** "⚡ ФОНОВАЯ ПАМЯТЬ" (lines 210–221): "память влияет не только на ответ после восприятия, но, возможно, и на само формирование того, что будет воспринято как значимое"; open question #6 of that edge (line 229): "Как фоновые ассоциации должны влиять на внимание, не превращаясь в скрытый фильтр подтверждения?" Separately, line 799 ("🚦 ДОСТАТОЧНО ИЛИ ГЛУБЖЕ"): "если система ошибочно решила НЕ углубляться, она часто никогда не узнает, что пропустила важное... эти ошибки могут не оставлять следа." And line 787, among "🌌 ОТКРЫТЫЕ ГОРИЗОНТЫ" (explicitly *not yet placed* in the map): "возможная переработка опыта без текущего внешнего запроса / offline consolidation."

**INFERENCE:** This is the same shape of problem as Finding 2 — a prior model (here, background associations rather than a user model) shapes what becomes a candidate for attention, and only attended-to material feeds the confirmation/revision loop. The document names this "скрытый фильтр подтверждения" and calls it an open "тайна," with no cross-reference to the structurally identical personalization worry. Crucially, the one mechanism that could plausibly rescue both loops — an offline/background process that revisits material that was *not* selected for deepening at the time, giving missed signal a second chance to surface — is explicitly listed as an unplaced open horizon (line 787), not a working part of the current architecture. And line 799 admits outright that a wrongly-skipped deepening decision may leave no trace at all.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (empirical/descriptive, institutional flavor):** A radiologist's background pattern-recognition, built on thousands of common-finding scans, pre-highlights statistically familiar regions. A genuinely novel pathology, unlike anything in her training distribution, never lights up any background association — it isn't misclassified, it's simply never promoted to the explicit "look closer" step, because that step only engages where something already registered as worth checking. An institutional audit months later finds a cluster of missed cases with a consistent unusual pattern; nothing in the ordinary workflow ever generated a discrepant expectation-vs-observation comparison, because the sufficient/deepen gate was never reached for that region at all.

**VERDICT:** unresolved. Credit for honesty: line 799 is a rare, explicit admission that this class of error may be structurally undetectable within the described architecture. But that also means the more optimistic language elsewhere ("leaves a channel open for the genuinely new" — lines 94, 583, 813) is claiming a capability the document itself, two sections over, says it cannot verify was actually exercised.

## Finding 4 — Speed/openness compatibility is stated as established in one place and reopened as unresolved two sections later

**SOURCE:** Line 94: "опыт должен помогать системе всё лучше понимать, где достаточно общей карты, а где нужно открыть детали. Быстрее ≠ поверхностнее; быстрее = лучше выбирать нужное разрешение." Line 583 repeats this almost verbatim. Line 819 (end of "🌱 ОПЫТ" section): "опыт может одновременно становиться предвзятостью. Поэтому остаётся открытым вопрос: как становиться быстрее и точнее, не становясь всё менее способным увидеть новое?"

**INFERENCE:** Lines 94 and 583 assert the equivalence flatly and without hedge ("быстрее = лучше выбирать нужное разрешение" — presented as a working characterization, not a hypothesis). Line 819 reopens the identical relationship as unresolved. Nothing in between supplies the missing argument for why faster recognition on the trained distribution doesn't trade off against sensitivity to the untrained region — the resolution-allocation reframing restates the goal ("choose the right resolution") without showing that the choice is itself acquired at zero cost to novelty-sensitivity.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (general/formal — bias-variance framing):** Any recognizer that speeds up on class A by sharpening features/priors tuned to A's distribution pays for that speed either through added capacity elsewhere or through reduced sensitivity to inputs unlike A. A spam filter that becomes faster and more confident on known spam patterns typically becomes, by the same mechanism, a worse alarm for a genuinely novel attack vector — the ordinary generalization trade-off, not a solved problem, and not one "faster ≠ shallower" makes go away by assertion.

**VERDICT:** rejected as stated at lines 94/583 (an unqualified equivalence asserted as settled); survives with scope once line 819's hedge is taken as the document's actual, more careful position. Recommend the document itself demote 94/583 to the same "research candidate, not established algorithm" status it explicitly gives the same claim at line 813.

## Finding 5 — The "not-found ≠ not-existing" guardrail family is strong as a stated norm but is not wired into the fast path it's meant to protect

**SOURCE:** The guardrail recurs independently at lines 105, 202–204, 740, 759–760, and 862 (NOT REPRESENTED ≠ ABSENT / LOSSY OMISSION ≠ GLOBAL NEGATIVE EVIDENCE), and again as РУТИНА ≠ НЕВАЖНОЕ (717–719). The worked failure case (lines 194–198) shows exactly how a discarded exception becomes, several inference steps later, "исключений нет" — with "никто не солгал" (no single step lied).

**INFERENCE:** As a value stated repeatedly across independent sections, this is a real strength of the document, not decoration. But every instance is a "must not"/"≠" assertion, not a check wired to a step. Nothing says whether the fast path (⚡ БЫСТРОЕ СХВАТЫВАНИЕ → 🚦 ДОСТАТОЧНО?) is required to consult a СЛЕД (line 490, "достаточный путь назад к основанию") before treating a pattern-match as sufficient, or whether that path can bypass the check entirely in the name of speed — which is precisely where the document's own worked example (194–198) says the slide actually happens.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (constitutive rules / protocol domain):** A chess rules-engine compiles "no piece may move through check" from a large case base; a rare en-passant/discovered-check interaction was never encountered and got compressed out. Asked directly about provenance, the system correctly says "unverified for this configuration" (satisfying the guardrail as a disclosure norm). But during an actual live game, the fast-grasping layer classifies the position as "routine, matches known pattern" and never triggers that disclosure at all — the guardrail is available on reflective query but not consulted on the ordinary fast route where the slide travels.

**VERDICT:** partially survives. Strong and repeated as a value; unaddressed as a working constraint on the specific fast/routine path the brief asks about. Per anti-reification, no new module is warranted — the precise relation needing clarification is simply: does СУТЬ+СЛЕД's "path back to grounds" attach to, or get bypassed by, БЫСТРОЕ СХВАТЫВАНИЕ's "sufficient, don't deepen" decision? The document currently leaves that undefined.

## Finding 6 — "Material deviation" is asserted as preservable but never given a criterion, and isn't even granted its own open question

**SOURCE:** Lines 715–724: "система должна сохранять способность заметить материальное отклонение от этой регулярности," illustrated only by "несколько последовательных наблюдений заметно уходят от привычного baseline." Compare legacy §6 (line 865), which offers a candidate-signals list rather than a formula (a correct anti-reification move). Contrast line 540 (cumulative-drift section), which at least proposes a tentative mechanism-candidate ("повторная диагностика старого понимания и сравнение ответов со старыми ответами").

**INFERENCE:** The document rightly refuses to turn "materiality" into a score. But the consequence is that the actual hard question — how much drift, over how many observations, counts as material rather than noise — is left not just unimplemented but unexamined: the worked example uses "заметно" (noticeably) to illustrate the very property it's supposed to explain, i.e., it restates the target rather than supplying an independent criterion or even a pointed research question. Unlike neighboring topics (e.g., line 552's explicit open question about choosing a discriminating question), this routine/baseline sub-topic gets no dedicated open-question entry of its own, and is never cross-linked to the cumulative-drift section that already has a tentative mechanism-candidate sitting right next to it in the document.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (medical/diagnostic + changing-world):** A lab value is stable for years, then drifts by a small, consistent amount each visit — structurally the same as the document's own "100 → 99.9 → 99.8 → ..." example (line 528), but that example lives in the *cumulative-drift* section, not the *routine-compression* section, and the two are never connected. Under routine compression, each single reading falls inside normal assay noise and matches the compacted baseline, so no individual observation trips the "noticeable deviation" the guardrail promises; only a comparison across many compressed instances would reveal the trend — exactly the comparison routine-compression is meant to make cheaper to skip.

**VERDICT:** partially survives. The refusal of false-precision scoring is right; the gap is that the document doesn't hold this sub-topic to its own usual standard of naming it as an explicit open question, and misses an easy, no-new-primitive fix: cross-reference the cumulative-drift section's mechanism-candidate into the routine-compression section, and add "what counts as material deviation from a compacted baseline" to that section's open-questions list.

## Closing: what held up vs. what's genuinely thin

**Checked and found NOT to be a real issue:**
- **Memory vs. Experience** — cleanly and repeatedly distinguished (line 916, restated at line 1006: "MEMORY = что осталось доступно. EXPERIENCE = как прошлое изменило будущее понимание"). This is a working distinction, not an empty label.
- **Selection boundary vs. possibility boundary, at the level of stated principle** — genuinely one of the document's stronger through-lines; the "NOT FOUND/REPRESENTED/RETRIEVED/ACTIVE ≠ absent/false/excluded" family recurs independently across at least five separate sections in both layers, which suggests deliberate reinforcement rather than a single lucky sentence.
- **Internal loss vs. external evidence** — I found no passage where the document itself treats its own compression as evidence of the dropped thing's real-world unimportance; every instance I located gets this the right way round (LOSSY OMISSION ≠ GLOBAL NEGATIVE EVIDENCE, NOT KNOWN RELEVANT ≠ KNOWN IRRELEVANT). Where this is thin is enforcement on the fast path (Finding 5), not the principle itself.
- **Blanket "experience is unambiguously good for openness" reading** — not the document's considered position; line 819 explicitly reopens it. The overclaim exists only in two early, unhedged sentences (94, 583), not as the document's sustained stance.

**Found genuinely thin/broken:**
- The four-way conflation of readiness / hard resource scarcity / immediate-action selection / learning selection (Finding 1) — no dedicated open question addresses concurrent competing demands on a finite budget.
- The self-confirming loop at both the personalization edge and the background-memory edge (Findings 2–3) — correctly flagged as open, but genuinely unblocked, and the one plausible fix (offline consolidation revisiting unselected material) is explicitly deferred as an unplaced horizon rather than connected to the loop it could address.
- The register inconsistency on speed/openness compatibility (Finding 4) — an unhedged claim sitting a few sections away from its own explicit reopening.
- The missing criterion (or even open-question status) for "material deviation" in routine compression (Finding 6), despite a ready-made candidate mechanism sitting uncited in a neighboring section.
