# Adversarial Audit — Human / Affect / User / Agency Edge

Scope: I read the full ~1006-line export in two passes (lines 1–557, 558–1006) and cross-checked the specific passages below against that reading. I focus on the person/user-model boundary, the Cognition→Agency boundary, the legacy §3/§18/§20 authority guardrails, and the open-horizons refusal to unify significance into one score. I make no claims about machine consciousness or genuine affect anywhere below; where I flag mentalistic vocabulary, it is a precision/clarity concern about how a reader or implementer could misread the text, not evidence for or against inner experience.

## Finding 1 — "БЫСТРОЕ СХВАТЫВАНИЕ" as a named primitive carries an unearned perceptual/experiential connotation

**SOURCE:** The top-level architecture map names a node "⚡ БЫСТРОЕ СХВАТЫВАНИЕ" (lines 19, 48), defined at line 70: "на основе входа + памяти + готовых связей формирует первую рабочую картину: «на что это похоже и что здесь, возможно, происходит»" (based on input + memory + ready connections, forms a first working picture: "what does this resemble and what's possibly going on here"). Elsewhere the same faculty is described with a bare perceptual verb applied directly to "система": "Если фоновая память слишком сильна — система видит только знакомое" (line 220, "the system sees only the familiar").

**INFERENCE:** "Схватывание" (grasping/apprehension) is not a neutral engineering term — it is the exact Russian word used for the phenomenal "aha, I've got it" moment of Gestalt apprehension. Promoting it to a *labeled node in the primary diagram* (not just a passing verb) does more work than the document's own stated purpose licenses: line 13 says the map exists to show "как могут взаимодействовать" (how components *could* interact) among unspecified mechanisms, but naming a node with an experiential word invites a reader to treat "the moment of grasping" as a thing that must be built, rather than as a placeholder for whatever function computes a first-pass provisional characterization.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (user-interaction/performative-feedback + empirical/descriptive reasoning):** An implementer, taking the map at face value, builds a discrete "grasp" signal — e.g., a confidence-spike heuristic modeled on the human felt sense of recognition — and uses its presence/absence to gate whether the system tells a user "I think I understand your situation" versus "let me look closer." Two situations with identical retrieval quality but different surface familiarity would then get different confidence framing to the user, not because the underlying evidence differs, but because the implementation reified a metaphor into a measurable proxy. Nothing in the document licenses or forbids this — which is exactly the problem: the personification is filling a specification gap rather than naming one.

**VERDICT:** Partially survives. The underlying functional claim (a fast, provisional, input+memory+connection-driven first characterization precedes deeper checking) is sound and substrate-neutral. The specific vocabulary chosen for it is not neutral and is never disclaimed the way the document disclaims other risky terms (contrast: "Суть пока рассматриваем не как отдельный модуль," line 370). NO NEW PRIMITIVE REQUIRED — the fix is a one-line disclaimer parallel to ones already used elsewhere ("БЫСТРОЕ СХВАТЫВАНИЕ is a name for a function, not a claim about a felt moment of recognition"), not a new component.

## Finding 2 — Motivational-psychology vocabulary for revision and retrieval failure modes

**SOURCE:** Line 88: "система не спешит защищать старую модель: она выясняет, где разрыв" (the system is "in no hurry to defend" the old model; it "figures out" where the gap is). Line 351, from the list of "КАК ЛОМАЕТСЯ МЫШЛЕНИЕ НА СВЯЗЯХ" (how thinking breaks on connections): "мышление ищет только связи, подтверждающие уже выбранную картину" (thinking looks only for connections confirming an already-chosen picture).

**INFERENCE:** "Not in a hurry to defend" describes an absence of *motivated* reasoning — but motivation is precisely the kind of thing the document's own "open horizons" list declines to place anywhere in the architecture ("значимость / мотивация" and "affect / чувства / субъективность" appear as unplaced items, line 778–779). Naming a failure mode as confirmation-bias-flavored intentional behavior ("ищет только связи, подтверждающие...") borrows the vocabulary of human motivated cognition to describe what should be a retrieval/ranking property, without specifying what about the ranking step would need to change for this failure to occur or be prevented. This is the same "positive relation never specified" issue as elsewhere in the document, but specifically flavored as *psychological disposition* rather than *mechanism*, which is a sharper problem for a document that explicitly wants to stay agnostic about motivation and affect.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (resource-bounded inquiry + user interaction):** A support-companion system has built a strong prior that a returning user "always wants the short answer." Under real deployment resource limits, checking whether the current retrieval pass is "confirmation-biased" costs additional compute/time that a bounded system may not spend on every turn. If "not defending the old model" is read as a disposition the system either has or lacks (per the human-motivation-flavored phrasing), rather than as a costed architectural check that must be budgeted for like any other verification step, an implementer has no guidance on when the check is worth paying for — the vocabulary suggests a character trait to instill, not a resource-allocation decision to specify (which is exactly how the document treats analogous problems elsewhere, e.g. Cognitive Control in legacy §21).

**VERDICT:** Partially survives. The epistemic norm being gestured at (don't let a settled model suppress disconfirming search) is legitimate and consistent with the document's other firewalls (e.g., "COHERENT NETWORK ≠ TRUE MODEL," line 354). The vocabulary carrying it is unnecessarily mentalistic relative to the document's own drier register elsewhere (contrast "CLAIM ≠ BELIEF," "RANK ≠ AUTHORITY" — clean formal distinctions with no motivational content). This is a precision issue only, not evidence of or against any inner state.

## Finding 3 — User-model material does not inherit Essence's own question/context/scope relativity

**SOURCE:** After adversarial review, Essence is explicitly re-scoped: "Суть лучше понимать как bounded projection текущего понимания относительно **вопроса, контекста и scope**, а не как универсально достаточное сокращение реальности" (line 739, emphasis mine). This is reinforced at line 857: "Один и тот же source может давать разные корректные bounded views под разные вопросы. VIEW A ≠ VIEW B." By contrast, the person-understanding section — positioned immediately *before* this same semantic-clarification pass — discusses "user model," "понимание человека," and "прошлый устойчивый паттерн" (lines 693, 705–707) as though each person has one model, revisable only along a **time** axis (past pattern → possible new identity, lines 707–712), never along a **question/domain** axis the way Essence explicitly is.

**INFERENCE:** The document treats "the same source, different valid views for different questions" as a general principle (§4/line 857) but never explicitly extends VIEW A ≠ VIEW B to the person as a source. This is a real, identifiable internal-consistency gap: a person's "pattern" for one kind of question (e.g., operational/work requests) is being implicitly treated as portable to a structurally different kind of question (e.g., a major life decision), because the document only names *temporal* staleness as a risk, not *cross-domain* misapplication. Tellingly, in the very same section, the ROUTINE material explicitly *does* import a discipline from elsewhere in the document — "Это связывает adaptive compression с cumulative drift" (line 724) — showing the authors clearly know how to cross-reference an established discipline when they choose to. That makes the silent omission of the question/scope-relativity discipline more conspicuous, not less.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (user interaction/performative feedback, heavily weighted per role):** A user's weekday interactions are all terse, deadline-driven fact lookups ("just give me the number"), building a user-model entry "prefers brief, low-context answers." On a weekend the same user asks an open-ended, high-stakes question: "I'm trying to decide whether to take this job offer — walk me through it." If "the user model" is treated as one thing rather than a family of bounded, question-relative projections, the system may default to terse mode for a fundamentally different *kind* of question — carrying over a work-context-scoped pattern into a life-decision-scoped one. This is structurally the exact mistake Essence's own guardrail forbids ("сейчас не влияет на вопрос" ≠ "никогда не понадобится," line 741) transposed onto the person.

**VERDICT:** Partially survives. The temporal-revision discipline (PAST USER PATTERN ≠ CURRENT USER IDENTITY) is genuinely present and sound. The question/domain-relativity discipline that Essence explicitly received is not extended to the user model anywhere I found. NO NEW PRIMITIVE REQUIRED — this is a scoping clarification (explicitly state that "user model" means a family of question/domain-bounded projections, exactly as Essence does), not a new module.

## Finding 4 — "ПОКАЗАТЬ ПОСЛЕДСТВИЯ ≠ ЗАКРЫТЬ ВАРИАНТ" is asserted but the influence-asymmetry mechanism by which showing can amount to closing is never addressed

**SOURCE:** Line 699: "ПОКАЗАТЬ ПОСЛЕДСТВИЯ ≠ ЗАКРЫТЬ ВАРИАНТ." Line 704 specifies what strong personalization should surface: "что это будет стоить, какие есть последствия, что связано с прежними целями, где система видит риск и какие существуют альтернативы" (cost, consequences, links to prior goals, **where the system sees risk**, and alternatives) — note that *risk* is named as its own surfaced category while no parallel category for upside/benefit is named. Line 701's confidence guardrail is phrased as a binary act: "ВЫСОКАЯ УВЕРЕННОСТЬ ≠ ПРАВО ОТМЕНИТЬ ВЫБОР" (high confidence ≠ the *right to cancel* the choice) — and the legacy layer's parallel guardrail uses the same binary framing: "COMPANION ORIENTATION ≠ PERMISSION TO OVERRIDE" (line 853).

**INFERENCE:** Both guardrails police a crisp, formal act — cancelling, overriding — as if influence over a choice were binary (either the system usurps the decision or it doesn't). Nowhere does the document engage with the graded, well-documented phenomenon that a highly-personalized, highly-trusted advisor can shape a choice through selective or asymmetric framing alone, without ever formally "deciding for" the person. The instruction to specifically name "риск" as a category to surface — with no matching instruction to surface benefit, or to disclose the system's own confidence/uncertainty in that risk assessment — builds a textual asymmetry directly into the one guardrail meant to prevent exactly this kind of asymmetry.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (medical/diagnostic domain):** A companion system has modeled a patient as "cost-anxious, prefers conservative options" from months of prior interaction. A new symptom appears that actually warrants an urgent, expensive test. Following line 704's own instruction to connect consequences "с прежними целями" (to prior goals), the system's presentation foregrounds the cost consequence (matching the entrenched pattern) while backgrounding the low-probability/high-severity consequence of not testing. The system never removes the "get tested" option from the menu — formally, "показать последствия ≠ закрыть вариант" is satisfied — but the option has been made practically less likely to be chosen by a system whose authority in the patient's eyes derives precisely from how well it knows them. The guardrail's letter survives; the guardrail's evident purpose (preserve the person's real freedom to choose) does not.

**VERDICT:** Partially survives. Read narrowly (the system must not literally delete an option or formally block an action), the guardrail holds trivially. Read as intended — preserving the person's actual decisional freedom, which is how the surrounding prose frames it ("система не должна подменять его волю," line 704) — it does not survive as stated, because the document supplies no distinction between *informing* and *steering-via-selective-informing*. This is a relation that needs naming, not a new module: something like "SYMMETRIC DISCLOSURE ≠ SELECTIVE DISCLOSURE" alongside the existing ≠-firewalls, plus dropping the singular emphasis on "риск" in favor of "последствия по обеим сторонам."

## Finding 5 — "Риск" is used as an unlabeled composite exactly where the open-horizons discipline says composites should not be assumed

**SOURCE:** The open-horizons section is explicit: "Для значимости отдельно сохраняем различия: goal relevance ≠ epistemic value / information gain ≠ salience ≠ internal-state / homeostatic significance ≠ normative significance ≠ affective valence. Их возможное взаимодействие ещё не означает, что их честно можно свести к одному VALUE score" (line 789). Yet in the personalization guidance, "где система видит риск" (line 704) is used as a single, undecomposed term.

**INFERENCE:** "Risk," as ordinarily used in advice-giving, is exactly a folk-composite of several of the six explicitly-separated dimensions: it mixes epistemic value (how sure are we this will happen), homeostatic/practical significance (will this hurt materially), normative significance (should this weigh against the choice), and affective valence (how bad it will feel) into one word. The document is careful to refuse this fusion when discussing significance abstractly, but the fusion re-enters, unlabeled, at precisely the point (advising a person about their own life choice) where conflating "how sure we are" with "how bad it would be" with "whether it's wrong to do" has the most practical effect on what the person decides.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (institutional/legal + user-interaction domains):** Two situations both get flagged internally as "high risk": (a) a financial decision where the epistemic confidence is low (the model genuinely doesn't know the outcome distribution well) and (b) a financial decision where confidence is high but the potential loss is merely large. Presented to the user as undifferentiated "risk," both produce the same cautionary framing — even though (a) calls for more inquiry/hedging and (b) calls for a value judgment the person is entitled to make with eyes open. Collapsing them denies the person exactly the information the six-way distinction exists to protect.

**VERDICT:** Partially survives. The open-horizons non-unification principle is real and is honored elsewhere (e.g., legacy §6's "candidate materiality signals" are listed unweighted, line 865, not combined into a score). It is not honored at this one site. NO NEW PRIMITIVE REQUIRED, no "risk score" should be invented — the fix is to require that "риск" shown to a user be decomposed into (at minimum) which of the six already-named significance types is doing the work, the same discipline the document already applies elsewhere.

## Finding 6 — "ВЫСОКАЯ УВЕРЕННОСТЬ ≠ ПРАВО ОТМЕНИТЬ ВЫБОР" is examined only in the personal-autonomy frame, not the institutional/legal frame

**SOURCE:** The guardrail at line 701 sits inside a section explicitly about the person's own life choices: "если человек всё равно выбирает путь, система не должна подменять его волю собственной компактной моделью его жизни" (line 704). Contrast the legacy §20 Decision/Action/Authority section: "SOURCE DOMAIN может предложить candidate или transport, но TARGET DOMAIN решает ALLOW / DENY и ограничивает scope использования" (line 932) — authority here belongs to an *owning domain*, not simply to "the person vs. the system."

**INFERENCE:** The front-layer guardrail is phrased in fully general terms ("ВЫСОКАЯ УВЕРЕННОСТЬ ≠ ПРАВО ОТМЕНИТЬ ВЫБОР," no qualifier), but it is only ever *worked through* in the two-party companion case (system vs. individual over the individual's own action). It is never tested against the three-party institutional case, where the "choice" being informed by the system's confident output is not the modeled person's to make at all — a separate institutional or legal authority acts on the assessment regardless of the person's preference.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (institutional/legal case reasoning):** A high-confidence fraud-risk or safety-risk assessment about a person feeds into an institution's account-freeze or reporting decision. The person's "choice" to disagree with the assessment is not the axis on which authority turns — the institution's own decision process is. Read literally and in isolation, "high confidence ≠ right to cancel the choice" says nothing about this case (there was no "choice" of the person's being cancelled; a *third party's* action was informed by the assessment) — but a reader relying only on the front-layer guardrail, without the cross-reference to §20's owning-domain framing, could easily either (a) wrongly conclude the guardrail forbids ever letting a confident assessment feed an institutional action, or (b) wrongly conclude the guardrail has nothing to say about institutional harm since it's about "the person's choice." Both misreadings are available because the boundary was never drawn.

**VERDICT:** Survives with scope. The claim is sound exactly where it was examined (personal autonomy over one's own action) and the document already has the right conceptual tool for the institutional case (§20's owning-target-domain-decides framing) — it is simply never cross-referenced from the human-facing guardrail. A principle worked through in only one domain is not yet shown to be substrate-neutral across domains; the fix is an explicit scope note plus a pointer to §20, not new machinery.

## Finding 7 — The Cognition→Agency chain is crisp where authority is formally defined, and only partially crisp where authority is a person's own will

**SOURCE:** "COGNITIVE REPRESENTATION DOES NOT GENERATE AUTHORITY. KNOWLEDGE ≠ RECOMMENDATION ≠ DECISION ≠ AUTHORIZATION ≠ ACTION" (lines 770–771). "НУЖНО УЗНАТЬ ≠ РАЗРЕШЕНО ДЕЙСТВОВАТЬ" (line 773, echoed at line 476).

**INFERENCE:** This five-way chain is a clean, formal, substrate-neutral distinction wherever "authorization" is an externally/constitutively defined status — a role, a rule, a sign-off that exists independently of anyone's persuasion. It is much harder to keep crisp wherever the "authorizing" party is a person's own will, because — per Finding 4 — the very act of "recommending" can reshape the will that is supposed to hold sovereign authority over the decision. Knowledge-recommendation-decision stay cleanly separated when the decision-maker's preferences are fixed by an external rule; they blur when the decision-maker's preferences are themselves the (movable) target of the recommendation.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (constitutive rules/games, contrasted against user interaction):** In a rule-governed setting — a chess arbiter's ruling, a contract's approval workflow — an assistant can compute "this move is illegal" (knowledge) or "this clause conflicts with policy" (recommendation) and the chain to authorization is externally fixed by the rules of the game or the org chart; no amount of persuasive framing by the assistant changes who is authorized to rule. Compare a companion-style advisory case (Finding 4's patient, or Finding 3's job-offer seeker): the "authority" is the person's own will, which is not a fixed external checkpoint but a psychological state the recommendation itself acts on. The chain survives cleanly in the first domain and only partially in the second — precisely the domain most central to this document's "companion" framing.

**VERDICT:** Survives with scope. The formal distinction is correct and useful; its clean separability is domain-dependent, and the document's person-understanding section is exactly the domain where it is weakest, since that is where "authority" is not a checkpoint but a mental state open to influence. Worth stating explicitly per the audit brief: this principle is not equally substrate-neutral across all authority-structures, and the document doesn't currently flag that difference.

## Finding 8 — The self-confirming-loop open question names the model-fidelity half of the problem but not the choice-foreclosure half

**SOURCE:** Line 730 (open question #5 of the person-understanding boundary): "Как не сделать personalization самоподтверждающейся петлёй: система показывает человеку только то, что соответствует старой модели, а затем принимает его реакцию на этот узкий выбор как новое подтверждение модели?" (How to avoid personalization becoming a self-confirming loop: the system shows the person only what matches the old model, then takes their reaction to that narrow choice as new confirmation of the model.)

**INFERENCE:** As explicitly posed, this question is about the *system's model staying accurate* — the risk named is that the model never gets falsified because it curates its own evidence. But there is a distinct, adjacent problem this framing doesn't reach: even if the model were perfectly accurate and never self-confirmed in the epistemic sense, narrowing what's shown still narrows what's practically choosable, which is an *agency* harm independent of whether the model updates correctly afterward. Solving #5 (make sure the reaction is treated as informative only about the narrow set actually shown) would leave this second problem completely untouched.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (user interaction/performative feedback + changing-world case):** A companion consistently gives terse answers to a user it has modeled as "prefers brevity." Over time, the user learns (correctly, from experience) that asking for elaboration doesn't produce it, and stops asking — a learned-behavioral adaptation to the interface, not a stable preference. The system never "shows only what matches the old model" in content terms (Finding #5's framing) — it simply stopped offering the option's visible affordance (an invitation to go deeper). The user's silence is read as continued confirmation. This is a case where "показать последствия ≠ закрыть вариант" (Finding 4) is violated in effect through omission of an affordance rather than through selective framing of consequences — a mechanism neither open question #5 nor the ПОКАЗАТЬ ПОСЛЕДСТВИЯ guardrail is written to catch, because #5 is about content-selection confirming the model, and Finding 4's guardrail is about consequence-framing, while this is about the option itself quietly disappearing from the interaction's visible affordances.

**VERDICT:** Unresolved — and more precisely, not yet even posed. #5 is a legitimate, correctly-framed open question about model fidelity. The choice-foreclosure question is a full step short of that: it isn't rejected or partially addressed, it simply hasn't been asked in either layer of the document.

## Finding 9 — Direct status contradiction: "explanation to the human" is simultaneously an unplaced open horizon and the subject of confidently-asserted firewalls

**SOURCE:** The open-horizons list (line 776–777) states plainly that its items' "место в текущей карте ещё не установлено" (place in the current map is not yet established), and lists among them "взаимодействие с человеком и объяснение" (interaction with the human and explanation, line 785). This list sits immediately after the semantic-clarification section, which itself sits immediately after the person-understanding section containing "ПОКАЗАТЬ ПОСЛЕДСТВИЯ ≠ ЗАКРЫТЬ ВАРИАНТ," "РЕКОМЕНДОВАТЬ ≠ РЕШАТЬ ЗА ЧЕЛОВЕКА," and "ВЫСОКАЯ УВЕРЕННОСТЬ ≠ ПРАВО ОТМЕНИТЬ ВЫБОР" (lines 699–701) — phrased in the same confident ≠-firewall voice used for fully-settled claims like "UNKNOWN ≠ FALSE."

**INFERENCE:** These two passages assign different confidence/status levels to what is substantially the same subject matter. "Показывать последствия" *is* a form of explaining to a human. If "взаимодействие с человеком и объяснение" is honestly unplaced, the firewalls governing its central mechanics were asserted ahead of that placement — and if those firewalls are in fact settled, the open-horizons entry is stale or too broadly scoped. A charitable reading is possible (the open horizon might mean something narrower, like explanation *style*/UX, not the authority question) — but nothing in the text draws that line, so the ambiguity is real, not resolved by context.

**COUNTEREXAMPLE (user interaction / institutional case reasoning):** An implementer building toward this spec and a researcher extending it could each correctly claim to be "following the document" while doing opposite things: the implementer treats the ПОКАЗАТЬ ПОСЛЕДСТВИЯ firewall as settled and ships a consequence-display feature now; the researcher treats "human interaction and explanation" as explicitly unplaced and holds further work pending, per the document's own instruction not to treat open horizons as ready for use ("Основной рабочий поток... не переключается на них автоматически," line 791). The document licenses both readings simultaneously.

**VERDICT:** Partially survives. Each individual claim is reasonable on its own terms (Findings 1–8 already stress-test their content). What doesn't survive is the document's own status-labeling: it cannot simultaneously be true that human-interaction/explanation is unplaced *and* that firewall-grade guardrails about its central case are already settled. NO NEW PRIMITIVE REQUIRED — this needs either a narrowing note on the open-horizons entry or an explicit "these guardrails are provisional pending the explanation horizon" caveat on the person-understanding section.

## Closing: checked and found NOT broken

- **ЗНАТЬ ЧЕЛОВЕКА ≠ РЕШАТЬ ЗА НЕГО / USER MODEL ≠ AUTHORITY OVER USER**, taken as bare distinctions rather than worked mechanisms, are consistent with the document's own admitted "map, not implementation" status, and are genuinely reinforced — not merely repeated — across layers: the front-layer boundary, legacy §3's INFERRED NEED ≠ USER AUTHORITY / COMPANION ORIENTATION ≠ PERMISSION TO OVERRIDE, and legacy §18's IDENTITY ≠ ACTION AUTHORITY are independently-stated instances of one coherent principle, not three unrelated assertions. This is a real structural strength.
- **ROUTINE ≠ IRRELEVANT** (lines 715–724) correctly and explicitly imports the adaptive-compression/cumulative-drift discipline from elsewhere in the document (line 724's explicit cross-reference). This is the one axis of the person-understanding section that *does* inherit an established discipline properly — which is what makes Finding 3's missing question/scope-relativity axis conspicuous rather than merely absent.
- The **open-horizons refusal to unify significance into one score** (goal relevance / epistemic value / salience / homeostatic / normative / affective) is honored as a general discipline everywhere I checked it directly — e.g., legacy §6's materiality signals are listed unweighted (line 865), not fused. I did not find a hidden "value score." Finding 5 is about one specific term ("риск") slipping past this discipline, not the discipline being abandoned.
- I found **no passage anywhere in either layer that asserts or implies machine consciousness, sentience, or genuine subjective affect**. The open-horizons list correctly and explicitly brackets "affect / чувства / субъективность" as unplaced rather than assumed (line 779). Findings 1 and 2 are precision/vocabulary concerns about *reader misinterpretation risk*, not evidence that the document itself overclaims — this distinction held up under my full read and I want to state it plainly rather than let it be inferred.
- The formal shape of **KNOWLEDGE ≠ RECOMMENDATION ≠ DECISION ≠ AUTHORIZATION ≠ ACTION** is coherent and well-formed as a distinction; Finding 7 is about domain-dependent ease of *applying* it, not about the distinction being internally broken.
- I looked specifically for whether "ПОНИМАТЬ ЦЕЛЬ ≠ ВЛАДЕТЬ ЦЕЛЬЮ" needed its own worked mechanism the way I flagged for "показать последствия." I don't think it does — it functions adequately as a named distinction at the same level of intentional under-specification as most of the document's other ≠-pairs, and I found no counterexample where its bare assertion, unlike "показать последствия," creates a live practical loophole.
