# Velantrim Substrate-Neutral Architecture

**Status:** Cross-project architectural orientation  
**Authority:** Descriptive bridge only; not a runtime, Canon, authority domain, or replacement for owning-project specifications.

## Core idea

Velantrim should be understood first as an architecture of **obligations to preserve meaningful distinctions**, not as an architecture of technologies.

Graphs, RAG, SQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite, vector stores, Python, LLMs, event sourcing, files, analog media, neuromorphic systems, and future substrates are implementation profiles. They may be replaced. The applicable semantic obligations may not be silently discarded without changing the architecture or explicitly declaring loss.

```text
TECHNOLOGY != ARCHITECTURE
CAPABILITY != AUTHORITY
SAME OUTPUT != SAME SEMANTIC CONFORMANCE
IMPLEMENTATION REPLACEMENT != PERMISSION TO COLLAPSE DISTINCTIONS
```

The durable blueprint therefore asks **what distinctions must remain representable, inspectable, scoped, revisable, and accountable**, rather than which tables, classes, models, databases, or frameworks must exist.

## Seven cross-project laws

1. **Source, representation, claim, evidence, belief, and knowledge remain distinct.** Promotion between them is never silent.
2. **Every meaningful position has scope.** Context, time, and authority boundaries must remain explicit; scope expansion must be declared.
3. **Unknown is not false.** Unsupported is not false. Failure now is not proof of impossibility.
4. **Revision requires lineage.** Supersession is not deletion and is not proof that the superseded position was false.
5. **Conflict is exposed before it is resolved.** Detecting tension is not equivalent to resolving it.
6. **Authority is role- and domain-bounded.** Ability to execute an operation does not grant authority to declare truth, identity, consent, or cross-domain state.
7. **Accountability is bounded.** A receipt or record proves only what it explicitly establishes.

These laws are a compact orientation layer, not a substitute for the fuller Native Kernel and Mentaury-Kernel specifications.

## Project projections

### 💠 Crystal

Retrieval, ranking, relation discovery, or model output may surface candidates. Evidence admission, provenance, integrity, trusted state, and bounded canonical writes remain separate governed concerns.

### 🗿 Titan

Presence of an artifact, configuration, wiring, execution, and observed runtime behavior are different claims. Orchestration and capability do not create epistemic or cross-domain authority.

### 🌀 Mentaury Soul

Claims, beliefs, self-models, identity, relationships, commitments, interpretation, and action authority remain distinct. A model of a person is not the person; a narrative about self is not automatically verified state.

### 🌎 Continuum

The long-lived object is process state, not one replaceable inference instance or context window. Unknown or unresolved continuation state is a valid explicit state rather than a hidden failure.

### 🧬 Native Kernel

Native Kernel is the primary home for substrate-neutral semantic obligations, transition semantics, uncertainty, revision, provenance, identity/time, and conformance boundaries.

### 🪁 Mentaury-Kernel

Mentaury-Kernel is the primary home for cross-domain composition obligations: provenance preservation, scope, authority boundaries, declared loss, compatibility, and non-escalation.

## Conformance principle

Two implementations are not equivalent merely because they produce the same final answer. Equivalence must be declared for a scope and should require at least:

- preservation of the same applicable semantic obligations;
- compatible identity mappings or explicit classification of their differences;
- preservation of temporal and ordering meaning without invented causality;
- compatible treatment of uncertainty, conflict, revision, and lifecycle distinctions;
- preservation of provenance and authority boundaries;
- explicit representation of unknown, partial, unsupported, failed, and lossy cases where applicable;
- exclusion of known approximation/loss regions from overbroad equivalence claims;
- an explicit verification procedure.

A radically different substrate can therefore conform. A sophisticated modern implementation can fail to conform if it silently collapses a required distinction.

## Ownership and routing

This document does **not** centralize authority.

- **Native Kernel** owns its technology-neutral semantic laws and conformance obligations.
- **Mentaury-Kernel** owns cross-domain composition invariants and declared-loss semantics.
- **Crystal, Titan, Soul, and Continuum** own their project-specific semantics and may expose technology-neutral projections of them.
- **Velantrim System OS / Knowledge Atlas** may orient and route these ideas, but do not become a new semantic sovereign.

```text
shared principle != shared authority
orientation != Canon
cross-project vocabulary != ownership transfer
```

## Design test for future mechanisms

Before adopting a technology, ask:

1. What human/system capability is needed?
2. Which semantic distinction or obligation must survive?
3. Which project owns that obligation?
4. Can the mechanism implement it without collapsing scope, provenance, uncertainty, revision, or authority?
5. What loss does the mechanism introduce?
6. Is that loss explicit and acceptable for the declared scope?
7. Can a different future substrate satisfy the same obligations?

If yes, the mechanism is an implementation profile. It is not the architecture itself.
