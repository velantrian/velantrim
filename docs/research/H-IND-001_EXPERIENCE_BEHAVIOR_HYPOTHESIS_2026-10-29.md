# H-IND-001 — Can own experience produce durable behavioral differences?

**Working research hypothesis, 2026-10-29** · OPEN / NOT_TESTED · NOT CANON · NOT RUNTIME

Two identical initial LLM-based systems may develop stable, reproducible differences in later behavior when the observable consequences of their **own actually selected** actions differ. The measurable claim is functional behavioral individuation, not evidence of consciousness, subjective experience, changed model weights or human identity. Nondeterministic generation alone is not an indicator.

## Proposed experiment

Three randomized groups with matching initial configuration: (A) mainly successful outcomes; (B) mainly unsuccessful outcomes; (C) no-history controls. Before experience, capture independent baseline decisions. For A/B let the model choose an exploratory action, execute *only that action* in a bounded deterministic sandbox, present the observation, and durably store a provenance-bound episode. After a fresh local process starts, offer new tasks and record choices, strategy changes while retaining a goal, relevance filtering, false transfer, correction when evidence changes and appropriate uncertainty.

Preregister multiple independent replications and blinded oracles. Include matched no-history, unrelated history and equal-information-but-told-by-other controls; counterbalance labels, order and environment configurations. A failure to show robust differences is a valid result. Keep source facts, observation, interpretation, intent and action separate; UNKNOWN is not UNCHANGED.

## Current evidence boundary

Velantrim R V3.1.2 is now a **completed offline package**, not just the previously described proposed assignment: the independent audit reported 31/31 new and 24/24 historical + 80/80 prior offline tests passed and verified 105 payload hashes; **zero actual V3.1.2 model calls**. The new protocol distinguishes exploratory action from evidence-backed decisions and introduces matched A–G; however C-1's measured baseline still used a fixture (F5), and C-2's old episode was still synthetic (F6). Exact token matching and independent semantic human review are pending. No scientific causal own-history result is established.

First priority is a separate, bounded and explicitly approved **live** Velantrim R pre/action/outcome/restart/post diagnostic after F5/F6 conditional capture, not another round of purely offline tests. H-IND-001 is a later replication study, not an already executed experiment. No approval or spending authorization is implied by this documentation.
